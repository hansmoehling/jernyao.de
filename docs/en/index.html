Multi-station presses for cold forming

Bolt Formers · Nut Formers · Parts Formers — Jern Yao, Tainan

Jern Yao builds multi-station formers that do both: the precision of the big names and a price that pays off in the piece price. More than 5,300 machines in over 40 countries run on this principle; component machining, assembly and electrics are made entirely in-house in Tainan.

The backbone are the JBF bolt formers, grown up three- and four-die as the answer to the American Boltmaker: the bread-and-butter machines of the fastener plants. At the top stand the JBP long-stroke and JNP short-stroke formers with up to seven dies and 930 tonnes of forging force, built for the complex formed part.

For the German-speaking market, Hans Moehling represents the works exclusively: consulting, project engineering, spare parts and technical clarification — in a direct channel to Tainan.

Technology

Engineered for continuous duty

In the tooling area

  • QTC quick-change system: punch and die blocks are set up and preset outside the machine.
  • Fine adjustment of knock-out and stop in steps of 0.01 mm; HMI with motorised parameter setting.
  • Crane concepts for tool change: complete block or single tool.

In the works

  • Design backed by DEFORM-2D/3D forming simulation and FEM analysis of the frame.
  • Full depth of manufacture in Tainan: component machining, assembly and electrics in-house, nothing outsourced — core parts on the company’s own five-face milling centres.
  • Key electrical components from Germany, Japan and the USA; ISO 9001, CE by TÜV Nord.

Heritage

How it came about

The connection goes back to 1982: Moehling brought these machines, then still under the name Tung Kuang, to Europe and accompanied their way to today’s maturity. That work is carried on here.
